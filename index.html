Good questions welcome

A few things clients ask first.

Your situation is unique. These answers are a useful starting point; a quick conversation will make them specific to you.

Do you work with first-time home buyers?+

Absolutely. Patrick explains financing, neighborhoods, contracts, inspections, offer strategy, and closing in plain language—one step at a time.

Should I sell before I buy?+

It depends on your equity, financing, timing, and comfort with risk. Patrick can map both sequences so you understand the tradeoffs before deciding.

Can you help manage my rental property?+

Yes. The team supports tenant placement, rent collection, repairs, maintenance, and ongoing oversight for Bay Area rental owners.

Which communities do you serve?+

Doan & Associates is based in San Jose and works throughout Santa Clara County and surrounding Bay Area communities.
